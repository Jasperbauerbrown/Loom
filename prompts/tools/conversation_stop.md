Stop a thread you spawned, and every sub-thread under it. The thread's running step is interrupted, any approval it is waiting on is denied, and messages queued for it are discarded. It ends idle; nothing is deleted, and its worktree stays. Only your own threads (or their sub-threads) can be stopped. Use it for duplicates, wrong goals and work the person no longer wants; to redirect a thread that should continue, use `conversation_send`. Arguments: `conversation_id` (required, one per call; make parallel calls to stop several); `reason` (short, recorded in the thread's transcript). A later `conversation_send` reopens a stopped thread.
