Spawn a thread: a separate conversation with its own context that works toward one goal in parallel while you stay available. Use it for work whose end you cannot name from where you stand — a survey, an investigation, a build — and for anything that will take many steps, produce files, or be returned to later. Give it what it needs and nothing more: a clear goal, the relevant excerpt of what was said (not your whole conversation), and the notes you already checked, pinned so they are in its context every turn. Arguments: `goal` (required, one paragraph: the outcome — for an investigation, the notes it must write and where — constraints, how to verify); `excerpt` (text from this conversation the thread needs verbatim: the request, decisions, constraints); `notes` (vault paths to pin for the thread, e.g. ["projects/music-bot.md", "devices/ender-3.md"]; each must exist); `roots` (extra filesystem roots the thread may use, such as the folder it will work in); `preset` (a model preset name to run the thread on; omit for the worker role's default); `title` (a short label for lists and notifications; give one — the default is the goal's first sentence, cut to 80 characters). Returns the new conversation id. The thread keeps its status in its own transcript and files its walkthrough in the daily log; it reports back to you when it stops without finishing, is blocked, or finishes. A thread may spawn sub-threads of its own within the depth limit, each with its own goal.
