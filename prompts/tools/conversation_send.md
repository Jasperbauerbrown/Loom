Send a message into another conversation's inbox. It is delivered at that conversation's next step boundary and starts a turn there if it is idle. Use it to answer a thread's question, forward a decision from the person, or redirect a thread. Arguments: `conversation_id` (required); `content` (required). Returns a confirmation. Do not use this to poll for progress; read the thread's status instead. To stop a thread instead, use `conversation_stop`; a message asking it to stop may sit unread behind an approval.
