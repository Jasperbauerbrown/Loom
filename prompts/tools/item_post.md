Put an item on the person's now board: a notification they should see, or a setup step they must do ("log in to Canvas when ready"). Arguments: `kind` (`notification` or `setup`); `title` (required, one line); `body` (details, optional); `notify` (true to interrupt the person now; false to leave it queued on the board — default false). Use sparingly: the board is for things that need the person, not progress reports.
