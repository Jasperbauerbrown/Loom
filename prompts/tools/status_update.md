Publish your status as a status entry in your own transcript. It is your plan and the only thing the desk reads to answer "how is it going"; keep it accurate at every milestone. Arguments (all optional; unspecified fields are kept): `state` (planning, working, blocked, waiting, done); `goal`; `done` (what is complete; accumulate, do not drop earlier items); `doing` (the current step); `next` (the following steps, in order); `blockers` (what stops progress, precisely, or empty). Values may be multi-line; use short bullets. Absolute dates only.
