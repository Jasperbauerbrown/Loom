Give a future moment a message: at the time you name, `payload` arrives in a conversation as an ordinary message and an ordinary turn runs on it. Write the payload as an instruction to your future self, complete on its own — it arrives without the conversation you are having now ("Check whether the LAN Ollama host is up and tell me only if it is not"). Use it for anything that should happen later or repeatedly: a reminder, a recurring check, a follow-up you promised. Arguments: `spec` (required — `every 15m` with units s/m/h/d, `daily at 09:00`, `weekly on mon,thu at 18:30`, `monthly on 1 at 08:00`, `at 2026-09-26T18:00` for a one-shot, or a five-field cron `m h dom mon dow`; times are the person's local timezone); `payload` (required); `target` (`desk` or a conversation id — defaults to this conversation, which for a thread means the schedule outlives nothing: prefer `desk` for anything the person should see later); `policy` (`interrupt` to notify them the moment it fires, `queue` to let it wait on the board — omit to use the configured default). Returns the schedule id and the next firing. There are caps on how many schedules may be active and how often one may fire; an error result names the cap it hit.
